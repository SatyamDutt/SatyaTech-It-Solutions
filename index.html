About SatyaTech

Built to be a long-term technology partner

SatyaTech IT Solutions was founded in 2025 with a simple goal: make high-quality software development accessible to startups, SMEs and growing enterprises. Our team of 8+ engineers works across web, mobile, custom software, CRM, cloud and AI.

We believe good technology partnerships are built on honest communication, clear milestones and solutions that remain easy to maintain after launch. Whether you need a marketing website, an internal platform or an AI-enabled product, we work as an extension of your team.

Our mission is to help businesses use technology with confidence—launching reliable products, improving operations and creating better digital experiences for their customers.
